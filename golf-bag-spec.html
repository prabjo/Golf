Woods

The Women's Darkspeed Max line is lofted 18.5° / 21.5° / 24.5° and stamped 3W / 5W / 7W. Functionally they play as a 5W / 7W / 9W, which is how they're named here.

05

Trim & weight

Cut a shaft and the head goes light. Work out what to put back before the club feels dead in your hands.

—

0.5″ trimmed = −3 swingweight points  ·  2 g added = +1 point  ·  full compensation ≈ 12 g per inch

    One session on the range

    The session travels in one direction: same club at one target to start, everything changing by the end. Set the bucket size and the phases scale with it.

    Before the first ball — five minutes, no ball. Run the swing-recall drill, then take three to five swings with every body cue dropped. That handoff is what stops drill mode leaking onto the course. Full sequence →
