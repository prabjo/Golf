The idea

Two things, one loop

Equipment and practice run in parallel and inform each other. Neither changes on a theory — both change on what actually happens with a ball in the air.

Fitting

Head feel drives tempo. If a club gives feedback, the swing takes care of itself.

Practice

Internal focus builds a movement off the ball. External focus is what performs it.

Both

Don't fix what's working. Put the unknown variable in the unproven club or the unproven cue — never both at once.

Testing rule: don't work on equipment and swing changes in the same session. Each needs the other held still to mean anything.
